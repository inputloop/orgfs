* spoke licensing

This is the spoke itself: one named place where the register of licences is kept.

Every file put into it is cut into 5 stripes, and each stripe is kept on 2 of the members, so one member can be lost without losing the file. The spoke is public: anyone may be shown what it holds and where it is held.
